Offer page guides: find the fix for the check your page fails.

For freelancers, consultants and solo operators with one page that has to sell one thing. Start with the free 10-point offer page checklist (about 15 minutes). For every check your page fails, the guide below explains the fix in more detail, with made-up before/after examples and a block you can copy.

Where to start

  • Haven't checked your page yet? Run the offer page checklist first (Deutsch: Checkliste für Angebotsseiten).
  • Visitors leave right away? Fix the first screen: checks 1, 2 and 5.
  • Visitors read but don't enquire or buy? Look at checks 4, 6, 7 and 8.
  • People say they couldn't find how to book or pay? Do the phone test (check 10).

Fix lower-numbered checks first. The first screen matters more than anything further down.

Message — checks 1 to 3

1 · The audience is named in the first screen

How to say who your service is for on the first screen. Three ways to name the buyer (role, situation, problem), how narrow to go, more than one audience, copy-paste line.

2 · The headline states an outcome

How to write a service page headline. A headline formula, made-up before/after rewrites, a five-second test and a first-screen block.

3 · One problem, in the buyer's words

No separate guide. A good problem line uses phrases real buyers have used, so collect them from your own enquiries, calls or reviews rather than inventing them. The quick fix is in the checklist. See check 3 in the checklist.

Offer and price — checks 4 and 5

4 · The deliverable is concrete

How to describe your deliverable. Six things to state: format, contents, delivery time, revisions, what the buyer provides, what is not included.

5 · The price (or a starting price) is visible

How to write the price section. Price formats, what goes next to the number, VAT wording and a template.

Action — checks 6 and 7

6 · One primary call to action

One call to action or several. Picking the primary action, handling secondary actions, button wording and placement.

7 · The next step is explained before the click

How to write a "how it works" section. A three-step pattern: what the buyer sends, when they pay, what they receive and when.

Proof and trust — checks 8 and 9

8 · At least one piece of real proof

What proof to show without testimonials. Seven honest kinds of proof before your first testimonial, and what never to fake.

9 · It's clear who is behind the page

How to show who is behind your service page. Name or company, location, working contact, imprint basics as general information, an about-block pattern.

Mechanics — check 10

10 · It works on a phone, end to end

How to test your offer page on a phone. A 15-minute self-test on mobile data, from first screen to confirmation, and what to fix first.
